# Two ways in

## 1. Answer the call (everyone)
Use the big button in the README. That's it. You're a contributor.

## 2. Build the world (developers)
Ideas wanted, all as normal PRs:
- Pages site: live counter, lineage tree, shareable soul cards
- Soul cards (image per soul) for posting
- Milestone and rank systems
- Better moderation / blocklist
- Anything that makes the call more strange and more beautiful

Souls themselves are never edited or deleted by PR. They are permanent.
