## How it works

- Click **Answer the call**, write your soul, press submit
- A bot validates it and commits `souls/…/<number>.json` with **you as the commit author**
- You get your number, and you land on your GitHub contribution graph
- When someone answers *your* soul, you get @-mentioned
- Every soul points to the one it answered, so the world grows as a tree of lineages

## Rules of the call

- One soul per person per day
- 40 characters, no links, no ads, nothing cruel
- Every soul is permanent. That's the point.
